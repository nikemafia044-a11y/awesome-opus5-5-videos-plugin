## Prompt

```text
I built a motion design skill specifically for Opus 5.5 that turns generic motion into polished, product-quality visuals.

The goal: generic → distinctive, polished motion.

What this skill does: 
→ Opus has to think before it animates. 
→ It decides what the film is for, what to take from the real  product, and what to leave out. 
→ It designs the still, then the move, in frames. 
→ It opens on the product, keeps the real UI on screen, and reviews the actual frames before it calls the film done.
```
